## Livox ROS messages and ROS C++ generation

`include/livox_ros_driver/CustomMsg.h` and `CustomPoint.h` were inherited from the FAST-LIVO2
base revision. They identify themselves as generated by `gencpp` from Livox ROS message
files. The original generation environment/version is not recorded in that base revision.

The [Livox driver license](https://github.com/Livox-SDK/livox_ros_driver/blob/master/LICENSE.txt)
expressly covers `CustomMsg.msg` and `CustomPoint.msg` under MIT, with copyright (c) 2019
Livox. Its complete original notice, including the accompanying dependency notices, is
retained as [`LICENSES/Livox-ROS-driver.txt`](LICENSES/Livox-ROS-driver.txt).

The ROS [gencpp](https://github.com/ros/gencpp) generator is BSD-3-Clause; its notice credits
Willow Garage, Inc. (2011). The original BSD notice is retained as
[`LICENSES/ROS-gencpp-BSD-3-Clause.txt`](LICENSES/ROS-gencpp-BSD-3-Clause.txt). These notices
record the origins of the generated headers; no complete Livox driver or ROS distribution
is vendored here.

(GPLv3, **unresolved compatibility with the GPLv2-only host**), Sophus at `a621ff2`, Eigen,

modification notice and is retained as a separate GPL-3.0-only patch with
[`LICENSES/GPL-3.0-only.txt`](LICENSES/GPL-3.0-only.txt). It grants no change to that
dependency's license.
